This is your designs folder.

When Tee Studio asks "Choose designs folder", pick THIS folder.
- Every design is saved here automatically as  <design-name>.tee.json
- Exports (PNG, PSD, PDF, SVG, JPG, mockups) go into  exports/<design-name>/

You can copy, back up, email or share these files. Put a .tee.json file in
this folder and it shows up under "My designs" in Tee Studio.
