TEE STUDIO — T-shirt design studio that runs from a folder
==========================================================

START
  1. Double-click  index.html  (use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge).
     No install, no server, no internet needed — everything is inside this folder.
  2. Click "Choose designs folder" and pick the  my-designs  folder next to index.html
     (any other folder works too). Chrome remembers it; on later visits it may ask
     you to click "Reconnect" once.
  3. Start a new design, start from the sample, or open one from "My designs".

SAVING
  - Work saves automatically into the designs folder while you draw (see the
    "✓ Saved" note at the top right). ⌘/Ctrl+S saves immediately.
  - File > My designs / New  shows every design in the folder.
  - Click the design name at the top to rename it.
  - Firefox / Safari can't write to folders: there, designs are kept inside the
    browser and exports download to your Downloads folder.

EXPORT  (Export button, top right)
  PNG     print-ready, transparent background, 72 / 150 / 300 DPI (300 DPI = 3600 × 4800 px)
  PSD     Photoshop file, one layer per object, groups as layer folders, names,
          opacity, blend modes and hidden layers kept. Optional: every layer as
          its own PNG, plus a hidden shirt-colour layer for previewing.
          Text arrives as pixel layers (not editable type) at the chosen DPI.
  PDF     12 × 16 in page at the chosen DPI, for print shops
  SVG     vector, fonts embedded, opens in Illustrator / Inkscape / CorelDRAW
  JPG     flat on white, for sharing
  Mockup  the design on the chosen shirt colour, 2000 × 2100 px

PHONES & TABLETS
  The layout adapts: on tablets the panels slide in from the right (▤ button),
  on phones the tools sit along the bottom (⋯ shows the less common ones), the
  panels open as a sheet from the bottom and the menus are behind ☰.
  Pinch with two fingers    zoom (the point between your fingers stays put)
  Drag with two fingers     move around the page
  Long-press                right-click menu (objects, nodes in the Shape tool)
  Double-tap                double-click (Focus mode on a group, Shape tool...)
  Palette                   tap = fill · press and hold = outline
  Phones and tablets save designs inside the browser (Android/iOS browsers
  can't write to folders) and exports download.

PICK TOOL  (V — works like CorelDRAW / TraceDraw)
  Click again on a selected object   rotate/slant handles: corner arrows turn,
                     side arrows slant, the ring is the centre of rotation (drag
                     it to turn about another point; double-click it to reset)
  Corner handle      scale in proportion   Shift: from the centre
                     Ctrl: whole 100 % steps   Alt: freely (no proportion)
  Side handle        stretch; drag past the other side to mirror
  Ctrl while moving  keep the move horizontal or vertical
  Turning/slanting   Ctrl: 15° steps
  Space mid-drag     drop a copy where the object is and keep going
  Right-click mid-drag  the original stays, a copy takes the change
  Esc mid-drag       cancel
  Right-button drag  shows where it goes, then: Move Here, Copy Here,
                     Copy Fill / Outline / All Properties onto the object below
  Right-click        context menu     Alt+click: the object underneath
  Alt + marquee      also take objects the frame only touches
  Tab / Shift+Tab    next / previous object      +  duplicate in place
  Ctrl+D             duplicate; again repeats what you did to the copy
                     (move it 2 in, Ctrl+D → the next one is 2 in further)
  Arrows             nudge 0.1 in · Shift 0.2 in · Ctrl 0.01 in

GROUPS, CLIP FRAMES (POWERCLIP) & FOCUS MODE
  Ctrl+click       the object inside a group under the pointer (round handles);
                   move, scale, colour or delete just that one. Ctrl+click again
                   goes one group deeper. Click elsewhere and the group closes.
  Double-click a group or clip frame   Focus mode: edit what's inside, the rest
                   fades to 20 % and can't be picked. The bar at the top shows
                   where you are (click a step to go back up, ✕ to leave).
                   Esc with nothing selected goes up one level.
  Place inside frame (Object menu / right-click): select the artwork, choose
                   Place inside frame…, click the shape (or text!) to hold it.
                   The contents are clipped to its outline; its outline is drawn
                   on top. Great for a photo inside big letters.
  Drag onto a frame holding W = put inside · holding K = replace its contents.
  Clip frame bar (top-left when a frame is selected):
                   Edit (contents shown whole, Finish puts them back),
                   Select contents, Extract, 🔗 lock contents to the frame
                   (off: the frame moves alone), Fit: centre / fit / fill /
                   stretch. Colours you pick go to the frame, not the contents.
  Create empty clip frame turns a shape into a placeholder to drop art into.
  Opacity mask (◐ on the bar): the frame is no longer drawn — its brightness
                   shows the contents (white = visible, black = hidden; ⇄ inverts).
                   Fill the frame with a white→black gradient for soft fades.
  Saving, undo and every export always see groups closed; Ctrl+click and
  Focus mode never change your file.

LIVE EFFECTS  (four tools in the toolbox, also in the Effects menu)
  Effects stay live: change, move, scale or edit the object and the effect
  follows. Each tool's settings are on the property bar (Apply / Clear /
  Break apart). Break apart (Ctrl+K) turns an effect into real objects.
  Drop shadow  drag from the middle of an object = flat shadow (offset = drag);
               drag from near an edge = perspective shadow standing on it.
               Handles: white square start, coloured square end, bar = opacity.
               Bar: presets (Soft, Tight, Lifted, Hard, Glow, On the floor,
               On the wall), offset/angle/stretch/fade, opacity, feather,
               feather direction & edges, merge mode (Multiply by default),
               colour. The Properties panel's Shadow list uses the same presets.
  Contour      drag outward from an object = outside, inward = inside;
               steps, offset, mitred/round/bevelled corners, colours blend to
               the end fill/outline (direct or round the colour wheel),
               object and colour acceleration. Great for sticker outlines.
  Envelope     click an object for a frame of 8 nodes; drag nodes (Straight,
               Single arc, Double arc or Free mode). Double-click the frame
               to add a node, a node to remove it. Presets: Arch, Bulge,
               Squeeze, Flag, Perspective, Circle. Mapping: Putty, Original,
               Horizontal, Vertical; Keep lines. Works on text (arched titles).
  Distort      drag on an object: Push & pull (drag right = push, left = pull),
               Zipper (amplitude, frequency, random, smooth, local) or
               Twister (turns and degrees). Drag the diamond to move the centre.
  Blend        drag from one object to another: live in-between steps (depth
               for titles: blend text to a darker copy behind it). Bar: steps,
               rotation, object/colour acceleration, colour direction, Loop,
               Sizing, New path… (steps follow a curve; Full path, Rotate all,
               Spacing). Move either object and the steps follow; delete the
               end and the blend goes. Break apart = steps become a group.
  Transparency Uniform (= opacity) or a Linear / Radial / Conical / Square
               gradient: drag across the object to set it, drag its nodes,
               double-click the line to add a node (or a node to remove it).
               Merge modes (Multiply, Screen, Add, Overlay…), and Apply to
               fill only / outline only / both.
  "To curves" (or Convert to curves) makes an envelope or distortion permanent;
  Weld/Trim use the shape as drawn. Exports: PNG/PDF/mockup as drawn; PSD
  layers include the effect; SVG gets contours and warps as paths and flat
  shadows as blur filters.

SNAPPING & ALIGNMENT GUIDES  (magnet and guide buttons in the top bar)
  Moving, scaling and drawing snap to other objects' edges and centres and to
  the print area (within 10 screen pixels), shown by magenta lines.
  Equal spacing: a shape snaps halfway between its neighbours, or to the gap
  they already keep. Equal sizes: resizing snaps to another object's width or
  height. Alt+Q snapping on/off · Alt+Shift+A guides on/off.

MOVING AROUND THE PAGE
  Mouse wheel / trackpad scroll   pan        Ctrl/⌘ + wheel or trackpad pinch  zoom
  Space + drag, or middle button  pan        two fingers on a touch screen     pinch / pan
  View › Fit to window (or ⤢) brings the whole shirt back.

USEFUL KEYS
  V pick · R rectangle · E ellipse · S star · L line · P pen · F freehand
  B brush · T text · I eyedropper · Z zoom · F9 mockup preview
  ⌘/Ctrl: Z undo · ⇧Z redo · D duplicate · G group · ⇧G ungroup · C/V copy/paste
  Delete removes · Esc cancels
  Palette: left-click = fill, right-click = outline

SHAPING  (Objects panel › Shaping, the Object menu, or the bar at the top
          when 2+ shapes are selected — just like CorelDRAW)
  Weld             merge the selected shapes into one outline
  Trim             cut the bottom shape with the shapes above it (cutters stay)
  Intersect        new shape from the overlap (originals stay)
  Simplify         remove the parts of each shape hidden behind shapes above it
  Front − back     keep the front shape minus everything behind it
  Back − front     keep the back shape minus everything in front of it
  Combine          join into one object; overlaps become holes
  Break apart      split a multi-part shape into separate objects
  Convert to curves  (Object menu) turns a rectangle/ellipse/star into an editable path
  The bottom-most selected shape is the "target": results take its colours.
  Works on shapes, text, pen/freehand paths, groups and imported SVGs
  (text is turned into its letter outlines). Photos can't be shaped.

SHAPE TOOL — NODE EDITING  (F10, second button in the toolbox)
  Click a curve to see its nodes (□ cusp, ○ smooth, ◇ symmetrical, ▲ start).
  A rectangle, ellipse, star, polygon or line: double-click it to make it a curve.
  Text: Object › Convert to curves first.
  Click node           choose it      Ctrl+click add/remove   Shift+click a run
  Drag on empty space  marquee        Tab / Shift+Tab next/previous   Ctrl+A all
  Drag a node          move the chosen nodes (Ctrl or Shift: straight only)
  Drag a handle        reshape; smooth nodes keep handles in line,
                       symmetrical also equal length (Ctrl/Shift: 15° steps)
  Click a segment      choose it; drag it to bend (the point you grab follows)
  Double-click         on a segment: add a node   on a node: delete it
  + / −  Delete        add node / delete nodes (or the chosen segment) — the
                       curve is refitted so it keeps its shape
  C  S  Y              cusp↔smooth, symmetrical↔smooth, symmetrical
  Arrows               nudge nodes 0.1 in (Shift 1 in, Ctrl 0.01 in)
  Bar / right-click    Join, Break, Close (extend to close), To line,
                       To curve, Reverse, Extract subpath
  Drop an end node onto another end node to join them.

TEXT ON A CURVE
  Curve slider   (property bar, text selected) bends one line of text into an
                 arc: right = arch, left = smile. It re-fits as you type.
  Fit text to path  (Text menu) select a text and a shape or line (Shift-click
                 both). On a closed shape the text sits centred on top, upright
                 and outside. The bar then offers Place (top centre, bottom
                 centre, start, centre, end), On (baseline, centre, ascender,
                 descender), Offset along the path, ⇅ flip side and Straighten.
  Convert to curves (Text or Object menu) turns text into editable outlines —
                 every letter stays its own piece, no fonts needed any more.
  SVG export can also convert all text to outlines (tick the option); text on a
  curve is always exported as outlines.

SHARING THE APP
  Zip or copy the whole tee-studio folder. Keep these together:
  index.html, app/, lib/, fonts/  (my-designs/ holds your own work).

Not built yet: stretch/rotate/reflect nodes, elastic mode, reduce nodes,
dragging text along its path with the mouse, editable text layers in PSD.
